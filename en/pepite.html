Visual identity and packaging · 2024

Pépite

RoleIdentity, packaging, photography
ContextSchool project, individual
Year2024

Brief

Create a visual identity and packaging for a truffle risotto.

Concept

The winding lines of the truffle, inside and out.

The identity draws on the pattern visible inside the truffle and the earthy colours associated with it. The loop of the “P” echoes it and becomes a mark on its own.

Process

From paper to packaging.

Hand sketches to explore the shape of the packaging and how to show the product.

Execution

Packaging that lets the product show.

The pattern wraps the sides of the packaging and leaves the front free for the name and the window. The back brings together the illustrated preparation instructions, nutrition facts and ingredients, in French and English.

It consists of a glass jar, a metal compartment and a cardboard box. Together they showcase the product while staying responsible: the cardboard is recyclable, and the jar and compartment are reusable.

Applications

An identity that extends to everything else.

The logotype, monogram and slogan dress everything around the product: advertising, the shipping box, posters and the website.
