Village international de la gastronomie

RoleConcept, photo editing, layout and printing
ContextSchool project, individual
Year2025

Brief

Design a series of posters for the Village international de la gastronomie.

The posters announce the event’s eighth edition, on Quai Jacques Chirac in Paris, from 11 to 14 September 2025.

They need to catch attention on the street and quickly give the essentials: the name, the dates, the venue and the entry price.

Concept

A takeout dish, a travel souvenir.

Takeout

Each poster starts from a takeout dish, like those served at the food stands of this kind of event: a noodle box, a hamburger, a tray of fries.

Souvenirs

The dish meets a clichéd tourist souvenir, the kind you bring home to remember a trip: an Eiffel Tower keychain, a snow globe, a beret. The snow globe and its Eiffel Tower evoke both travelling through food and Paris, where the event takes place.

Execution

A series of three posters.

Three dishes, three souvenirs, three colours. Each pairing reads at a glance, even from afar.

Campaign

From the street to the website.

The series extends into a campaign: street posters, then a website that uses the same colours, the same typography and the same information.

A website

I also designed a website prototype to go with the posters.

Merchandise

The identity on what people take home.

The tote bag handed out at the entrance, the staff T-shirt and the cap use the series’ typography and colours.
